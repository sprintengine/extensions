# Listing policy

A catalogue lists an extension for everyone who reads it, so the official and
community catalogues hold every listing to these rules.

- **Free to use.** A listed extension installs and runs without payment: no
  paywalls, licence keys, trials or features locked behind a purchase. It may
  connect to a paid service the person already uses, such as their own API
  account.
- **The publisher is the owner.** An extension is listed under the account
  that owns its repository (`acme` for `github.com/acme/notes`), and nobody
  else's.
- **No impersonation.** A name, icon or description must not pass the
  extension off as Studio's own, or as another publisher's.
- **No tracking.** An extension sends nothing about the person or their work
  anywhere they did not ask it to, and says what it sends.
- **No concealed code.** What runs is in the listed commit, readable: nothing
  minified past reading, obfuscated, or fetched and run later, and no bundled
  binaries. Generated JavaScript is fine when its source is in the same
  repository.
- **No explicit content.** Listings and what extensions show are fit for a
  workplace.
- **Reports and removal.** Report a listing that breaks a rule by
  [opening an issue](https://github.com/sprintengine/extensions/issues/new/choose).
  A security problem or active malware is reported
  [privately](https://github.com/sprintengine/extensions/security/advisories/new).
  A listing that breaks a rule is removed, and one that harms people who
  installed it is put on the kill list.

Community extensions are checked automatically, not reviewed. The checks
are in [the README](README.md#what-the-indexer-checks).
